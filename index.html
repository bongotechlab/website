Ask a question
Do I have to buy every module?

No. Start with the modules you need, for example Inventory and Sales, and add Production, HR or Finance later. Everything connects when you do.

Can you change the software to match our process?

Yes, that is our main strength. We configure numbering, approvals, roles and locations, and we build custom screens, reports and modules for processes unique to you.

Cloud or on our own server?

Either. We can host it for you in the cloud or install it on your own server. Both include backups and updates.

Can we move our existing data from Excel?

Yes. Items, stock, customers, vendors and employees can be imported from Excel templates. We handle the cleanup and import during onboarding.

Does it work with our attendance machines?

Bongo ERP takes punches directly from ZKTeco terminals, and staff can also check in from the mobile app with GPS. Other devices can be integrated on request.

What support do we get after go-live?

A helpdesk, bug fixes, updates and refresher training, with optional annual maintenance plans for priority response.
