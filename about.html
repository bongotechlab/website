About us

Engineers who learn your business before writing code

BongoTechLab is a software company from Dhaka. We build Bongo ERP and custom systems for companies that have outgrown spreadsheets but don't want rigid foreign software.

Our story

Built on the factory floor, not in a slide deck

Bongo ERP started inside a real furniture manufacturer. We spent time with store keepers, line supervisors, delivery teams and accountants, and built what they actually needed: stock that matches the shelf, work orders the floor can follow, payroll that comes straight from the attendance machine.

That project taught us something simple. Every business is a little different, and good software should bend to fit it. Today we bring the same platform and the same approach to companies across industries.
