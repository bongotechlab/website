Modules

Everything a growing company runs on

Each module works on its own, and becomes more useful when connected to the others.

Inventory

Stock you can trust

Know what you have, where it is and what it's worth, at every location.

  • Item master with stock per warehouse, store and line
  • Goods receipt, issue, return, transfer and adjustment
  • Full stock ledger and reservations for orders
  • Cycle counts and reorder-level alerts
  • Stock reports with Excel export

Products & BOM

Know your real cost

Define what you make and what it takes to make it.

  • Product catalogue with versioned bills of material
  • Routing: the stages each product goes through
  • Live costing and "can we build it?" from current stock
  • Material requirements and where-used lookup
  • Excel import/export and website catalogue sync

Sales & Delivery

From quote to doorstep

Sell, deliver and collect, with proof at every step.

  • Customers, PDF quotations emailed and converted to orders
  • Sales orders, advance payments and receivables
  • Delivery trips, fleet and driver assignment
  • Customer OTP and proof of delivery
  • Printed challans and bills, with cash collected on delivery
  • Damage claims and sales KPIs

Production

See the floor in real time

Plan, track and cost every work order from cutting to finishing.

  • Work orders and a stage board for each line
  • BOM material reservation, issue and off-cut returns
  • Job cards and problem reports from the floor
  • Finished goods at actual cost
  • Line capacity, production KPIs and reports

Purchase & Vendors

Buy right, pay right

Control spending from request to payment.

  • Vendor master with performance and price history
  • Requisitions with approval limits
  • Purchase orders emailed to vendors, with delay tracking
  • Gate entry and goods receipt straight into stock
  • Vendor bills with 3-way match, payments and payables ageing

HR & Payroll

People, time and pay

From the attendance machine to the bank file, without retyping.

  • Employee directory, profiles, documents and letters
  • ZKTeco attendance push, plus mobile check-in with GPS
  • Shift rosters (weekly, monthly or custom) and overtime
  • Leave requests, balances and approvals
  • Salary sheets, approval, payslips, and bank, bKash and cash files

Machines & Assets

Less downtime, clear books

Keep machines running and fixed assets accounted for.

  • Machine status board, daily run-time and OEE
  • QR labels, breakdowns and preventive maintenance
  • Spare parts issued from inventory
  • Fixed asset register, custody, repairs and disposal
  • Straight-line depreciation runs and renewals

Finance

Numbers you can act on

Costs and profit, updated as the business moves.

  • Expenses with approval limits
  • Cost centres and monthly budgets
  • Monthly P&L, with revenue booked on delivery
  • Product margins and profitability
  • Receivables and payables ageing, revenue trend and month close

Dashboard, Reports & Settings

The view from the top

One home screen for the whole business, plus the controls to run it.

  • A live dashboard, global search and notification bell
  • Reports you can run on screen, export to Excel or receive by scheduled email
  • Company, locations, document numbering and integrations in Settings
  • Users, roles, permissions, approval limits and a full audit log
  • Sign-in with a password or mobile OTP
